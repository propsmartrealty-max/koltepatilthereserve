## 1. Statutory FEMA & RBI Guidelines for NRI Homebuyers

Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) and Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs/OCIs) holding a valid Indian passport or OCI card are legally entitled to purchase unlimited residential properties in India under general permission granted by the Reserve Bank of India:

1. **Funding Sources**: Payments must be remitted into India through standard international banking channels via:
   * **NRE Account** (Non-Resident External Rupee Account - Fully repatriable).
   * **NRO Account** (Non-Resident Ordinary Rupee Account - Subject to USD 1 Million annual remittance limit under LRS).
   * **FCNR Account** (Foreign Currency Non-Resident Account).
2. **Repatriation of Sale Proceeds**: When an NRI sells the property, the principal sale proceeds (up to two residential properties) can be remitted abroad freely without tax penalties, subject to standard long-term capital gains tax clearance (Form 15CA/15CB).
