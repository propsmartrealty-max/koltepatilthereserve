## 2. Why Sinhgad Road is Outperforming in 2026

Historically perceived as a busy commuter corridor, **Sinhgad Road** has undergone an unprecedented infrastructure upgrade that redefined its property market:

1. **The Operational Sinhgad Road Elevated Flyover**: Stretching from Rajaram Bridge to Fun Time Multiplex, this flyover bypasses major intersections, allowing residents of Vadgaon Khurd to reach Swargate Metro and Deccan Gymkhana in **just 10-12 minutes**.
2. **PMRDA 128-KM Pune Ring Road Corridor**: Passing directly adjacent to Vadgaon Khurd and Nanded Fata, providing high-speed bypass connection to Hinjewadi IT Park (25 mins) and the Pune-Bangalore Highway (NH-48) without touching city center traffic.
3. **The Mutha Riverfront Sanctuary**: While western Pune corridors like Hinjewadi and Wakad struggle with vehicular air quality and concrete density, The Reserve at Sinhgad Road offers **21.03 acres of pristine river-facing greenery**, direct views of the National Defence Academy (NDA) hills, and over 70% open landscape.
