# The Definitive Buyer's Guide to The Reserve by Kolte-Patil – Vyana, Sinhgad Road

When **Kolte-Patil Developers Ltd** unveiled **The Reserve** along the scenic banks of the Mutha River at Vadgaon Khurd on Sinhgad Road, it marked a monumental shift for South-West Pune's residential real estate. For decades, the micro-market was defined either by hyper-dense mega-townships or standalone standalone redevelopments. 

**The Reserve** introduces a rare category: **a 21.03-acre low-density, masterplanned riverside sanctuary**, featuring a multi-tiered **19,750 sq.ft. clubhouse**, an **84 ft. x 27 ft. resort swimming pool**, and panoramic vistas of the **National Defence Academy (NDA) hills**.

This comprehensive master guide provides homebuyers and investors with verified, factual insights into the project's layout, carpet areas, legal clearances, transit corridors, and pricing structure.

## 3. Structural & Architectural Specifications

The structural integrity of The Reserve is engineered to withstand seismic zone requirements while providing acoustic and thermal insulation:
* **Framework:** Monolithic reinforced concrete structure cast with precision Aluform aluminum formwork.
* **Flooring:** Glazed vitrified tiles (Kajaria or equivalent) in living, dining, bedrooms, and kitchen; anti-skid ceramic tiles in balconies and bathrooms.
* **Plumbing & Sanitary:** Concealed CPVC/UPVC pipelines with premium CP fittings and sanitaryware from Jaquar or Kohler.
* **Electrical:** Concealed copper wiring with modular Schneider/Legrand switches; provision for high-speed fiber-to-the-home (FTTH) and inverter backup.
* **Windows & Railings:** Powder-coated heavy-gauge aluminum sliding windows with mosquito mesh; toughened safety glass with stainless steel handrails on balconies.
* **Smart Access:** Digital biometric door locks on main entrance doors and video door phones connected to central security.

## 5. Locational Advantages & Transit Multipliers

The Reserve is strategically situated in **Vadgaon Khurd**, directly at the sweet spot between Sinhgad Road's urban conveniences and natural riverfront seclusion.

* **Adjoining Upcoming Pune Ring Road (PMRDA 128 km Corridor):** Direct highway access reducing travel times to Hinjewadi IT Park to ~25 minutes and Pune International Airport to ~40 minutes, completely bypassing core city bottlenecks.
* **Sinhgad Road Elevated Corridor:** The 2.5 km flyover significantly cuts congestion between Rajaram Bridge and Fun Time Multiplex.
* **Swargate Underground Metro Station (15 mins):** Connecting the Sinhgad Road belt to Civil Court, Shivajinagar, and Pune Railway Station.
* **Major Education Institutes:** Minutes from Sinhgad Institutes (Vadgaon & Ambegaon campuses), Podar International School, and Abhinav Vidyalaya.
* **Healthcare Proximity:** Navale Hospital, Smt. Kashibai Navale Medical College, and Deenanath Mangeshkar Hospital (15 mins).

## 8. Pricing Guidance & Home Loan Facilities

The Reserve by Kolte-Patil offers transparent, RERA-compliant pricing. All-inclusive costs encompass:
* Agreement Value (Base price + floor rise + premium view charges)
* Stamp Duty (6% in Pune: 5% Stamp Duty + 1% Local Body Tax)
* Registration Charges (1% capped at ₹30,000)
* Goods and Services Tax (GST at 5% for ongoing construction)
* Society Formation, Water & Electricity Infrastructure, and Advance Maintenance.

Leading public and private sector banks—including **State Bank of India (SBI), HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, and Axis Bank**—have pre-approved the project for seamless home loan sanctioning with competitive interest rates and construction-linked payment plans (CLP).
