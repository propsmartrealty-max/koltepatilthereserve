Positioned along a serene river edge with scenic views of the **NDA Hills**, **Vyana at The Reserve** represents the latest residential benchmark by **Kolte-Patil Developers Ltd** on Sinhgad Road, Pune.

## 1. Project Masterplan & Phase 1 Scope
- **Master Development**: 21.03 Acres of integrated riverside development.
- **Phase 1 Land Parcel**: 6.95 Acres dedicated to Vyana (Towers T5, T6, T7).
- **Tower Elevation**: Ground + 21 residential storeys (8 units per floor, 487 total units).
- **MahaRERA Registration**: Registered under **PM1261012600748** (`Vyana Phase-I-T5, T6, T7`).

## 2. Thoughtful Space Architecture
The residences are built using **Full Aluform monolithic concrete construction**, ensuring earthquake resistance, damp-proof longevity, and acoustic insulation.
- **Slab-to-Slab Height**: 3.05 meters (~2.9 meters clear ceiling height).
- **2 BHK Carpet Area**: **791 sq. ft.** with dedicated utility balcony and cross-ventilation.
- **3 BHK Carpet Area**: **1,016 sq. ft.** with spacious master suite, living-dining foyer, and panoramic river-facing deck.

## 3. World-Class Clubhouse & Lifestyle
- **19,750 sq. ft. Contemporary Clubhouse** with step seating architecture.
- **84 ft. x 27 ft. Resort Adult Swimming Pool** and **14 ft. x 18 ft. Kids Pool**.
- **Pickleball Court**, outdoor sports turf, and reflexology paths.
- **Central Landscaped Boulevard** and dedicated children's adventure play zones.

## 4. Location Connectivity Matrix
- **DMart**: ~1 KM (2 mins)
- **Orchids The International School**: ~4 KM (8 mins)
- **Sinhgad Hospital**: ~4 KM (8 mins)
- **Sinhgad Institutes**: ~5 KM (10 mins)
- **Chandani Chowk**: ~10 KM (18 mins)
- **Pune Railway Station**: ~15 KM (25 mins)
- **Proposed Pune Ring Road & Metro Corridor**: Adjoining site boundary.
